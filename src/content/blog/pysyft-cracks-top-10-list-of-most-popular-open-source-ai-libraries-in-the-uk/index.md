OpenMined’s PySyft has been recognized as the 9th most popular open-source AI library in the UK, according to OpenUK’s 2024 State of Open report. This achievement highlights PySyft’s growing prominence in the open-source AI ecosystem and its essential role in advancing privacy-enhancing technologies.

The report showcases the UK’s dynamic open-source community, emphasizing the importance of these technologies in driving digital and AI innovation. Libraries like PySyft are celebrated for fostering transparency, collaboration, and impactful contributions to the global tech landscape. PySyft’s focus on enabling secure, privacy-preserving machine learning aligns closely with OpenUK’s vision of democratizing technology to create a fairer, more innovative digital economy.

Being ranked among the top AI repositories not only affirms the technical excellence of PySyft but also highlights the dedication of its global developer community. This recognition is a testament to the collective efforts of contributors working towards creating open and secure AI solutions.

At OpenMined, we are proud to see PySyft acknowledged in this esteemed report. It reinforces our mission to empower individuals and organizations to harness the power of AI while safeguarding privacy.

For more details, explore the full _State of Open_ report [here](https://openuk.uk/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/State-of-Open-The-UK-in-2024-Phase-2-Report.pdf) and join us in building the future of open-source AI.Visit [PySyft on GitHub](https://github.com/OpenMined/PySyft) to learn more and contribute to this impactful project.
