## ************Interview with************ Daniel Huynh

Github: [@dhuynh95](https://github.com/dhuynh95%20)  |  Twitter: [@dhuynh95](https://twitter.com/dhuynh95%20)

********Where are you based?********

> “Paris, France.”

********What do you do?********

> “CEO of [Mithril Security](https://mithrilsecurity.io/), a startup specialized in secure inference of AI models.”

********What are your specialties?********

> “Data science, confidential computing, writing.”

********How and when did you originally come across OpenMined?********

> “I came across OpenMined in 2019, when I saw a meetup organized in Paris.”

********What was the first thing you started working on within OpenMined?********

> “I started writing articles on homomorphic encryption with a series on CKKS.”

********And what are you working on now?********

> “I am preparing two series, one on the basis of confidential computing, the other one on how to leverage confidential computing for deploying privacy preserving models.”

********What would you say to someone who wants to start contributing?********

> “Teaching is the best way to learn. It’s never too soon to start writing on a subject, you might not fully grasp it at first, but trying to explain it to others will not only help them but help you.”

****Please recommend one interesting book, podcast or resource to the OpenMined community.****

> “Because I am biased towards confidential computing now, I would recommend watching the webinar from the Confidential Computing Consortium:[https://confidentialcomputing.io/webinar-outreach/](https://confidentialcomputing.io/webinar-outreach/)“
