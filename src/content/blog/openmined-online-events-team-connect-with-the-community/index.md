The newly created Events Team at OpenMined will focus on online events – everything from open meetups to presentations and webinars.

As we have entered a new reality where in-person events are not possible, we have adapted and re-shaped meetups so we can stay connected.

Joining an online event could help you learn something new, connect with other OpenMineders, and it’s a fun way to be introduced to the many different parts of the community.

### Join events

All upcoming events hosted by the Events Team will be posted in the #community\_events channel.  
The events posted will be open for everyone to join and are free to attend.  
All events will also be posted on our online events calendar at [calendar.openmined.org](https://4mber.github.io/om-public-calendar/), with events open to the public listed in green.

### Volunteer

Would you like to help out organizing and planning an online event?  
If you are interested in joining the team as a volunteer, join the channel #community\_events and one of the Volunteer Intro and Onboarding Meetups that will be hosted regularly.  
If you have ideas for an online event you would like to create and host, reach out to @Helena Barmer on Slack.

### Monthly Paper Session Online Event

The Paper Session Team will provide monthly online paper session events. This team is part of our Events Team, and is led by Zumrut Muftuoglu.  
If you want to join one of the presentations keep an eye out in the channel #community\_events or our online events [calendar](https://4mber.github.io/om-public-calendar/).  
Want to be a speaker at the monthly paper session online event?  
Add your interest to [**this**](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdQny8JEU1XEGGEbKEAnt2M8cdMvnQxqS4uQGb3m2lyKtL4qw/viewform) form.  
For any questions reach out to @Zumrut Muftuoglu on Slack.

### Need help hosting an event?

We can help you host your online event with a Zoom webinar or Zoom meeting setting.  
If you have a presentation that you need help hosting add it to [**this**](https://airtable.com/shrRtpp78CUPFPSiQ) form.  
For any additional questions reach out to @Helena Barmer on Slack.
