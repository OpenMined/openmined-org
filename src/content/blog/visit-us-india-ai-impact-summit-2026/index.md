OpenMined is participating in the [India AI Impact Summit 2026](https://impact.indiaai.gov.in/) in New Delhi, in partnership with the [Human Genome Project II (HGP2)](https://hgp2.org). Together, we’re demonstrating how privacy-preserving infrastructure can enable countries to participate in global AI-driven genomics research on their own terms, without centralising sensitive health data.

We’re hosting three events across the Summit week.

---

## Summit Session: Genomics, AI, and the Future of Health: Data Visitation to Empower the Global South

-   Monday 16 February, 2:30–3:25 PM IST
-   [Bharat Mandapam](https://maps.app.goo.gl/gJWhue7TpiGPvdJp7), Room 15, New Delhi

AI and genomics are transforming public health, but the benefits remain unevenly distributed. Data sovereignty requirements, ethical obligations, and cross-border restrictions prevent genomic datasets from being used collectively for research and AI development.

Data visitation flips the model: instead of centralising sensitive data, algorithms travel securely to data held within national or institutional boundaries. This session demonstrates data visitation works in practice through [BioVault](https://biovault.net/), with examples drawn from real-world AI-enabled health system implementation

## From Framework to Implementation: A Policy Meeting on Data Visitation for Genomics and Health

-   Tuesday 17 February, 10:30 AM–12:30 PM IST
-   [India International Centre](https://maps.app.goo.gl/6e56Wj2nrsRKuSTr5), New Delhi

Building on the outcomes of the Summit session, this closed working session brings together policymakers, researchers, and technologists to refine a Data Visitation Implementation Framework, aiming to move from shared understanding to shared decisions on policy, governance, and operational pathways. The outcomes of this discussion will directly inform subsequent pilot activities.

The discussion will focus on translating policy principles into clear governance and implementation parameters, including sovereignty, consent, auditability, and access control, with particular attention to alignment with national law, public health missions, and public trust considerations.

_This session is by invitation only._

-   Tuesday 17 February, 1:30–5:00 PM IST
