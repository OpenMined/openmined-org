Are you ready to explore the exciting world of Federated Learning (FL) and other Privacy Enhancing Technologies (PETs)? The **30DaysOfFLCode Challenge** is here to inspire, support, and reward your journey into this technology. Whether you’re new to FL/PETs or already a pro, this challenge offers a unique opportunity to build innovative projects, share your progress, and connect with a global community.

## What Is the 30DaysOfFLCode Challenge?

The 30DaysOfFLCode Challenge is an asynchronous, self-guided 30-day initiative designed to help participants dive into Federated Learning by committing to daily progress. You’ll create FL projects, share updates on social media, and develop a deeper understanding of how this technology enables privacy-preserving machine learning across distributed data.

Here’s how it works:

1\. **Learn/Build:** Work on FL projects that excite you. Whether you’re experimenting with OpenMined tools, creating/consuming tutorials, or contributing to open-source libraries, your creativity sets the path.

2\. **Share:** Post daily updates about your journey on platforms like X (formerly Twitter), LinkedIn, or GitHub. Sharing your progress fosters accountability and inspires others to join.

3\. **Grow:** Explore resources, connect with mentors, and engage with the OpenMined community to grow your skills.

### How to Register

Joining the challenge is simple! Head over to [**30daysofflcode.com**](https://30daysofflcode.com) to learn more and register. Once signed up, you’ll receive everything you need to get started, including tips for joining the community, setting goals, sharing progress, and accessing resources.

#### Let’s Build the Future Together

Federated Learning is reshaping industries by enabling secure, collaborative, and privacy-preserving AI. With the 30DaysOfFLCode Challenge, you have the chance to play a key role in advancing this technology.

Are you ready to take on the challenge? Start today, and let’s build the future of FL together!
